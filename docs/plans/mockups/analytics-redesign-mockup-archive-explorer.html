About this data. Wait times are the figures each facility posts publicly, collected every minute from the official edwaittimes.ca feed and archived since May 2026. "Typical" ranges are per-facility medians for the same hour and day-of-week over the past 28 days. Posted waits are estimates made by the facilities themselves — sicker patients are always seen first. Facilities that don't publish wait times are excluded from regional numbers, not counted as zero.
